# Prompts ChatGPT · piezas del rig v1 (prueba de movimiento de 5 s)

Uso: chat nuevo en ChatGPT y adjuntar SIEMPRE `approved-references/v1-01-hoja-personaje.png` y `v1-06-accion-movil-vibra.png`. Primero se pegan las REGLAS FIJAS y después cada asset en un mensaje distinto (una imagen por asset). Si algo sale distinto, se pide `Repeat exactly the same, but fix: …` solo para esa pieza. Las imágenes se descargan en PNG y Claude las recorta, limpia y aprueba.

Puesta en escena de la prueba: Ratopín de rodillas, en 3/4 mirando a la DERECHA, aprieta con la llave inglesa (brazo lejano levantado) una abrazadera de la tubería. La mano cercana descansa en la rodilla y el móvil está en la funda de la cadera cercana.

## REGLAS FIJAS
```
You are drawing production assets for RATOPÍN, an original 2D animated series made with cut-out animation (like Toon Boom Harmony or Moho rigs). The two attached images are the APPROVED model sheet. Match them exactly in every asset:
- Same character: big round ears at the same spacing, large expressive eyes, long snout, black nose, two big front teeth, spiky hair tuft, whiskers, long segmented tail.
- Same outfit: white short-sleeve T-shirt, grey denim overalls with bib pocket and two metal buckles, rolled-up cuffs, leather tool belt with rectangular buckle and pouches, laced work boots.
- Same style: hand-drawn graphite pencil, dark outline of consistent weight, light hatching, black and white only. No color, no gradients, no cast shadows, no glow, no text, no labels, no numbers, no frame.
- Never redesign, restyle, simplify or "improve" him. If something is unclear, copy the model sheet.
- Asset sheet rules: every piece on a transparent background (if not possible: pure flat white #FFFFFF), clean closed outline around each piece, at least 80 px of empty space between pieces, no piece touching or overlapping another, all pieces at the same scale, flat orthographic view, even lighting.
Confirm you understand with one line and wait for the first asset.
```
